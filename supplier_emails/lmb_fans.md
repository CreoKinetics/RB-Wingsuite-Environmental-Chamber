## User requirement sent

- Approximate mass flow: 0.18-0.20 kg/s.
- Approximate dynamic pressure: 0.75-0.8 kPa.
- Estimated blower pressure capability desired: 1-3 kPa at low-density chamber condition.

## LMB preliminary response

Density scaling:

- 476 l/s at 1000-3000 Pa at density 0.412 kg/m3 corresponds to 476 l/s at 2912-8737 Pa at density 1.2 kg/m3.

Hyperfan 160:

- Can generate 476 l/s at 1500 Pa at density 1.2 kg/m3.
- Equivalent approximately 510 Pa at density 0.412 kg/m3.
- Not enough pressure.

MX150-01:

- 200 l/s at 4000 Pa at density 1.2 kg/m3 = 200 l/s at 1360 Pa at density 0.412 kg/m3.
- 270 l/s at 3000 Pa at density 1.2 kg/m3 = 270 l/s at 1020 Pa at density 0.412 kg/m3.

Interpretation:

- Two MX150-01 units may meet the flow target if arranged appropriately and if system pressure loss is near 1 kPa.
- Likely insufficient if system pressure loss is 2-3 kPa.
