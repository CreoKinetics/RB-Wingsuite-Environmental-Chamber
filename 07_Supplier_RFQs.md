# Supplier RFQs / Email Snippets

_Last updated: 2026-06-07_

## Statebourne ask

Ask for service/check, hose 3701004/3701006 advice, outlet adapters, hose relief advice, phase separator recommendation, service parts, PPE/O2 alarm options, manual/schematic.

Key points:

- Used Cryostor 60 serial number is recognised by Statebourne.
- Statebourne were not concerned by age and can support spares and onsite service/checks.
- Preferred hose length 1.2-1.5 m; avoid >2 m unless necessary.
- Ask whether in-line relief tee 9911009 is recommended for hose/Cryostor side, while noting HEROSE 1.5 barG reliefs are planned on external valve train.
- Ask about insulated hose option, but standard flexible stainless hose with strengthening sleeve is probably adequate unless price delta is small.
- Ask whether phase separator is useful for initial open dispensing/testing only, not necessarily inline before sealed heat-exchanger coil.

## HEROSE ask

Confirm/order 2 x USS-06002.0200.0000 pre-set at 1.5 barG.

Quoted relief valve spec:

- cryogenic safety valve, angle type, brass, PN63;
- size/connections: 1/4 in BSPT male x 3/8 in BSPT female according to ISO 7/1;
- 6 mm orifice;
- carbon-filled PTFE valve seal, closed bonnet;
- set pressure 1.5 barG;
- temperature range -196 to 150 C;
- cleaned/degreased for oxygen service;
- approved for air gases, vapours and cryogenic liquefied gases including LNG;
- standard PED marking.

Ask for:

- cryogenic needle/metering valve;
- manual cryogenic shut-off/globe valve;
- local pressure gauge;
- tees/adapters/reducers for short supported external valve train;
- 3/8 in BSPT relief outlet fittings/elbows to direct discharge safely.

## Schubeler / EDF supplier ask

Commercial framing:

- low-volume engineering/test-rig use, not 100s-off production;
- likely one unit initially if the engineering case is sound;
- do not lead supplier toward a multi-fan solution because multiple ~GBP 2k fans plus power electronics is unlikely to be commercially suitable;
- ask whether a single DS-51-DIA HST package is viable; if not, ask for the smallest suitable single-fan alternative.

Application:

- fixed ground-test environmental chamber, not aircraft installation;
- recirculating dry air/nitrogen;
- chamber pressure about 24 kPa absolute;
- air temperature down to -55 C;
- density about 0.40-0.412 kg/m3;
- target airspeed 120 kt TAS / 61.7 m/s;
- nominal test section about 93-100 mm;
- required flow about 0.47-0.49 m3/s for 100 mm, or about 0.419 m3/s for 93 mm;
- estimated system pressure loss provisionally 1-3 kPa at chamber condition.

Key questions:

- whether a single DS-51-DIA HST is likely suitable at this density/temperature;
- corrected performance estimates at approx. 24 kPa absolute and -55 C;
- whether fan/motor can operate in cold, low-pressure airflow with inverter outside chamber;
- whether DS-51 950 kV or 1050 kV is better for static ducted chamber operation;
- if DS-51 is not suitable, smallest suitable single Schubeler fan;
- continuous or semi-continuous run time at 50%, 75%, 100% power;
- pressure-vs-flow, thrust-vs-flow or static duct data;
- long-life/special bearings;
- low-temperature bearing lubricant;
- strengthened/abrasion-resistant blades for possible frost/ice/debris and test-rig durability;
- inlet lip/bellmouth for open-duct/test-bench operation;
- material, magnet, adhesive, balance, bearing and thermal-cycle limitations at -55 C;
- recommended YGE ESC/controller and telemetry setup;
- YGE Aureus manual says batteries only and power supplies not allowed: ask whether any approved lab DC supply arrangement exists;
- motor/ESC thermal monitoring and shutdown protections;
- rotor containment/guarding/overspeed recommendations for fixed test rigs;
- quote recommended single-fan package: fan, YGE Aureus 185 or recommended ESC, inlet ring, wiring/connectors/mounting/control accessories and durability options.

Ask Laurie for:

- expected lead time;

- whether the two-fan arrangement is a standard configuration;

## EV-APF / EV-MPR industrial fan supplier ask

Ask supplier about selector outputs:

- EV-APF561: 1,872 m3/h at 5,062 Pa static, 2900 rpm, 20 C at sea level / 1.2 kg/m3.
- EV-MPR502: 1,746 m3/h at 4,407 Pa static, 2900 rpm, 20 C at sea level / 1.2 kg/m3.

Questions:

- price and lead time for EV-APF561 and EV-MPR502;
- whether EV-APF561 is comfortably within stable continuous operating region;
- whether they recommend EV-APF561, EV-MPR502, or a slightly larger model;
- whether any suitable larger EV-APF / EV-MPR / equivalent unit is available from stock or short lead time;
- full fan curve and datasheet;
- motor power, voltage, current, frame, insulation, IP rating and VFD suitability;
- inlet/outlet dimensions, overall dimensions, weight;
- whether bare fan / bare-shaft / belt-drive / motorless arrangement is possible;
- casing/impeller suitability for cold dry air/nitrogen at -55 C;
- advice on keeping motor outside cold/low-pressure boundary.

## Kongskilde TRL ask

Ask Kongskilde which TRL model is appropriate for a dry air/nitrogen environmental chamber rig, not material conveying.

Selection duty:

- chamber condition: 24 kPa absolute, -55 C, density about 0.40-0.412 kg/m3;
- target actual flow: 1,700-1,750 m3/h;
- standard-density equivalent: 1,745 m3/h at about 4,400 Pa static at 1.2 kg/m3.

Questions:

- recommend TRL 75, TRL 100, TRL 150 or other;
- confirm static pressure, not only total pressure, at 1,700-1,800 m3/h;
- cold dry air/nitrogen suitability down to -55 C;
- whether motor can be kept outside pressure/cold boundary using V-belt arrangement;
- availability of bare-shaft or motorless versions;
- price, stock/refurbished/used availability, lead time;
- fan curve, dimensions, motor details, inlet/outlet connection details.

## Blower seller ask

Ask for motor plate photo, fan plate/part number, inlet/outlet dimensions, running video, bearing noise/vibration confirmation, guards/starter/VFD availability, and whether both units are identical.

## LN2 fill supplier ask

Ask whether they will fill the used Cryostor 60 after Statebourne service/check, and what inspection/transport paperwork or relief-device dates they require.
